# IntentChord — Commercial Licensing

The covered original IntentChord source is licensed under [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE). Uses outside the license's permissions require a **separate written commercial license** from the relevant rightsholder.

**Commercial terms are negotiated independently for each transaction.** IntentChord does not impose a universal fixed price, a default revenue percentage, an automatic royalty, or a mandatory subscription.

A commercial agreement can address:
- covered code and exact version;
- internal commercial use, hosted services, client delivery, resale, redistribution or white-label arrangements;
- pricing, duration, permitted installations, scope and sublicensing;
- support, updates, warranties, liability and termination;
- brand rights, if separately granted.

Commercial permission for contributor-owned code depends on rights validly granted to the project, such as under an accepted [CLA](CLA.md). Third-party component licenses continue to apply.

The community license permits certain uses by qualifying organizations; these permissions are not removed by this explanation. Read the official PolyForm text for precise scope.

## Commercial inquiries

For a first non-confidential licensing inquiry, [open a GitHub issue in the official IntentChord repository](https://github.com/hesham-m/IntentChord/issues/new) with a title beginning **Commercial licensing inquiry**. Briefly describe the intended usage and indicate how you would prefer to arrange a private discussion.

**GitHub issues are public when the repository is public.** Do not post pricing details, identities of private clients, proprietary project material, personal contact information, credentials, or draft contracts there. A confidential channel and any binding terms must be agreed separately with the project owner. Opening an issue does not grant a commercial license or promise acceptance.

**This page is an explanation, not a binding commercial offer or signed license.** No email address, pricing schedule, or binding contract is implied by this initial contact route.
